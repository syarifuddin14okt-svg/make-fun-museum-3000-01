## The label

> Parking meter, c. 2023. Citizens fed this street shrine coins and received, in return, a small paper blessing permitting them to stand still.

A deadpan curator from the year 3000, misreading the parking meter as a street shrine: coins go in, a small paper blessing comes out, and the blessing permits you to stand still. True, absurd, and straight-faced — exactly what the judge weighs.
